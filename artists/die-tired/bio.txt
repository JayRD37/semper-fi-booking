DIE TIRED
Modern Rock · Reading, Pennsylvania

Die Tired is a modern rock band blending raw emotion with a contemporary edge. The band has appeared on multiple Top 10 FM and internet radio playlists, with airplay in more than 15 countries.

In 2025, Die Tired earned LDM Radio's Best New Artist title. Their road show has taken them across the U.S., where energetic sets and a direct connection with fans continue to bring new listeners in.

Die Tired is Matthew DeAngelis (vocals and guitar), Matt Orlando (guitar and vocals), Jamieson "Jim" Lee (bass), and Brandon Ballantyne (drums).
