THE TRAYNR BAND

"The loudest country band you've ever heard."

The Traynr Band has been tearing up stages alongside big names like Craig Morgan, Eli Young Band, Jason Boland, Scotty McCreery, Muscadine Bloodline, Pecos and the Rooftops, Tyler Braden, and many more. Known for their high-energy performances and powerful sound, they've quickly become a must-see act in Nebraska and throughout the Midwest.

Voted by iHeartMedia and Jack Daniel's as one of the 25 best bar bands in the country in 2023 for their song "Damn Good Place to Drink," The Traynr Band brings the party and cranks it all the way up, every show.

With a mix of original music by singer-songwriter Tony Traynor and country favorites known everywhere, the band will have you up on your feet singing all night long.
