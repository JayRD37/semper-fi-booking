THE TRAYNR BAND

"The loudest country band you've ever heard."

The Traynr Band has been tearing up stages alongside Craig Morgan, Eli Young Band, Jason Boland, Scotty McCreery, Muscadine Bloodline, Pecos & the Rooftops, Tyler Braden, and many more. Known for high-energy performances and a powerful sound, they have quickly become a must-see act in Nebraska and across the Midwest.

In 2023, iHeartMedia and Jack Daniel's named The Traynr Band one of the 25 best bar bands in the country for their song "Damn Good Place to Drink." The band brings the party and turns it all the way up at every show.

With original music written by singer-songwriter Tony Traynor and country favorites audiences know everywhere, The Traynr Band keeps the room on its feet and singing all night.
