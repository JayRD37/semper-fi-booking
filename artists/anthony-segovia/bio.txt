Anthony Segovia
Country Artist — Grand Island, Nebraska

Coming out of Grand Island, Nebraska, Anthony Segovia is an emerging country music artist known for his distinctive voice and engaging stage presence. His shows mix country covers with original songs.

Anthony started playing guitar at 15. After an eight-year break while serving in the United States Marine Corps, he returned to music and has been performing ever since.

His influences include classic rock, country, and Christian music, from George Strait to Morgan Wallen, Koe Wetzel, and Riley Green. With his voice and guitar, Anthony writes and performs songs built around real stories.
