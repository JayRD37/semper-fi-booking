BRIELLA STEINER
Country/Rock Artist • Singer • Songwriter • Performer

Briella Steiner is a rising country artist from Lincoln, Nebraska, known for her powerhouse vocals, high-energy performances, and modern country sound with a touch of grit. At 20 years old, Briella has quickly built a reputation as one of the Midwest's emerging female country artists.

Briella has performed at major regional festivals and venues including Country Drive Live Music Fest, HEROSTOCK Country Music Festival, Tank's Country, Country Stampede, and stages throughout Nebraska, Kansas, South Dakota, and Ohio.

Her festival credits include sharing lineups with Koe Wetzel, Dasha, BlackHawk, Lita Ford, Hinder, Creed Fisher, Frank Foster, Them Dirty Roses, Scotty Austin, Bayker Blankenship, and others.

Her original releases include “A Better Feeling,” “Good Boy,” “Hell of a Match,” and “Repeating History.” Whether performing with The Wicked Ones or as a solo artist, Briella brings a commanding stage presence and an authentic country voice.
