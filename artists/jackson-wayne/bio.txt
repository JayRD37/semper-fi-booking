JACKSON WAYNE
Country · Nebraska

Jackson Wayne is an up-and-coming Nebraska country artist bringing high-energy performances, an engaging stage presence, and a crowd-pleasing sound to venues across the Midwest. Blending the classic feel of ’90s country with modern country hits and storytelling, Jackson delivers an entertaining show for bars, festivals, fairs, and live music events.

As one of the youngest artists on the Semper Fi Booking roster, Jackson brings fresh energy, a passion for country music, and a performance built to keep audiences engaged.

Jackson Wayne — New-generation country with a classic country soul.
