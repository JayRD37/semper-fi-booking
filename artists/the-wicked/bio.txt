THE WICKED
Alternative Rock • Grunge • Hard Rock

The Wicked is an alternative rock band built on grunge, heavy guitars, loud drums, and a live show that hits hard from the first song.

Rough around the edges, wildly inappropriate, and fully convinced they belong exactly where they are, The Wicked brings a live show that feels less like a performance and more like getting dragged into the middle of the party. The band hits hard, melts faces, talks shit, jokes with the crowd, and treats the people in front of the stage like they are part of the night.

Musically, The Wicked lives somewhere between alternative rock, grunge, hard rock, and whatever happens when a group of musicians decides restraint is overrated. The songs are loud, the shows are unpredictable, and the personality is impossible to miss.

The Wicked is loud, dirty, personal, unfiltered rock and roll with absolutely no interest in behaving.
