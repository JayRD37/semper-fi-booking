ANYONE ELSE
Pop-Punk / Indie Rock • Omaha, Nebraska

Anyone Else is an Omaha-based rock band blending the punch of pop-punk with the melody and melancholy of indie rock. Led by vocalist Meghan Thompson, the band pairs driving guitars and big hooks with honest, personal songs.

The band has earned radio airplay in six states, surpassed 50,000 Spotify streams, and opened for Shim, Alyeska, and The Funeral Portrait.
