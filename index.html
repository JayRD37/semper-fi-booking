Semper Fi Artist

Anthony Segovia

Country · Grand Island, Nebraska

Anthony Segovia is an emerging country artist with a distinctive voice and a live set that mixes country covers with original songs.

He started playing guitar at 15. After an eight-year break while serving in the United States Marine Corps, he returned to music and has been performing ever since.

His influences run through classic rock, country, and Christian music—from George Strait to Morgan Wallen, Koe Wetzel, and Riley Green. With his voice and guitar, Anthony writes and performs songs built around real stories.

Semper Fi Artist

The Traynr Band

Country · Nebraska

“The loudest country band you've ever heard.”

The Traynr Band has shared stages with Craig Morgan, Eli Young Band, Jason Boland, Scotty McCreery, Muscadine Bloodline, Pecos & the Rooftops, Tyler Braden, and many more. Their high-energy shows have made them a must-see act in Nebraska and across the Midwest.

In 2023, iHeartMedia and Jack Daniel's named them one of the 25 best bar bands in the country for “Damn Good Place to Drink.” With original music from singer-songwriter Tony Traynor and country favorites audiences know everywhere, the band keeps the room on its feet and singing all night.
